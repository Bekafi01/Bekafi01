## 🧭 About Me

I am an **AI / ML Engineer** specializing in Natural Language Processing, Large Language Models (LLMs & RAG), and predictive statistical systems. My experience ranges from engineering from-scratch PyTorch Transformers and contributing to foundation models (e.g. **AmhariXLM-0.5B** at **Fideliz.ai**) to building production RAG systems with rigorous evaluation benchmarks and sub-millisecond vector retrieval.

- 🧠 **Foundation Models & NLP** — built from-scratch 60.5M PyTorch Transformers, contributed to AmhariXLM-0.5B at Fideliz.ai, tokenization pipelines (BPE/SentencePiece), and Ethiopic homophone folding
- 🔍 **RAG & GenAI Systems** — deterministic neighbor expansion for multi-page textbook retrieval, Pydantic structured citations (`CitedAnswer`), and RAG Triad automated evaluation harnesses
- 📐 **Forecasting & Statistical ML** — hierarchical time series (MinT reconciliation), uncertainty estimation (Split Conformal Prediction), and drift monitoring (PSI)
- 🚀 **Delivery & MLOps** — containerization with Docker, API serving with FastAPI/Flask, interactive UIs with Streamlit, and Hugging Face model deployment
